Scope: villa-bagnamare/homepage.html, single-file homepage. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: families, groups and couples planning a Lipari holiday. Action: request availability (dates, adults/children, flat) prepared as WhatsApp/email to the owners. Proof: owner photos, real sizes, beds, distances. Constraints: Italian only, no prices, no invented reviews, no draft ribbon.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Lipari's own two stones, white pumice and black obsidian, with the turquoise that only pumice-bed water has. Refuses the blue-and-white postcard hero with a row of equal flat cards.
OWN-WORLD: Pumice ground (chalky warm white, porous grain), turquoise #1fa5a8-family fields owning whole bands, obsidian near-black glossy slabs with a cold sheen and sharp faceted (conchoidal) edges for every form and number panel. Display: a cut, chiselled serif-free face with sharp terminals; body a quiet humanist sans. One long horizon line runs across the page as the structural rule (raise from indoor-weather-sun: one vast horizontal band, the long axis).
STORY: The visitor sees the villa on the water, understands there are two places (on the sea / in the centro storico) and five flats by size, and sends dates.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed photo of the villa on the Marina Lunga water, cut at the horizon by the page's long line; name set large on pumice above; an obsidian slab overlapping the photo holds the working availability form (arrivo, partenza, adulti, bambini, appartamento, Invia su WhatsApp).
FORM: Pumice & obsidian, position 6 of 7 grounded; seed:27c71818a46cc6ca. Raise from design annual plate section: photographs untinted and presented as plates with a credit line (place, flat).
Signature interaction: choosing a flat in the slab or clicking "Chiedi per questo" on a flat fills the slab; the obsidian sheen follows the pointer.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Adaptations (recorded after finish review): hero uses the view from the villa terrace instead of the villa seen from the water, citing PRODUCT.md "the sea is the product"; the villa itself leads the turquoise band as the drone plate. Display face is Chakra Petch (chamfered, cut terminals that echo the faceted slabs); Anybody was dropped as too round.
