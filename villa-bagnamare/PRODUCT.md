## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS: one self-contained `homepage.html` per site, images inlined, same format as `bio-bottega-sabina/homepage.html`. Proposal draft prepared by FattoFacile for the owners; no "bozza" ribbon (user's choice): the page should read as the finished site.

## Users
Families, groups of friends and couples planning a summer holiday on Lipari (Aeolian Islands), mostly Italian, many arriving by ferry/hydrofoil from Milazzo. They compare a few holiday flats, look at the sea view and the number of beds, then ask the owners for availability and price for their dates.

## Product Purpose
Holiday rentals run directly by the owners (Maria Catena and Agostino), two properties in Lipari:
- **Villa Bagnamare** (via Bagnamare 15, Marina Lunga): Aeolian-style villa on the sea, renovated May 2022, ~700 m from the centre and the ferry port, 300 m from Porto Pignataro, bus to Canneto beach <5 min.
  - **Eolo 1**: ~120 m² + ~45 m² sea-view terrace (part covered patio), 4 bedrooms (two 4-bed, two 3-bed), 2 bathrooms, 1 kitchen, up to 14 guests; ceiling fans + air conditioning (installed 2025).
  - **Eolo 2**: two-room flat ~50 m², private sea terrace, Aeolian-style kitchen, double bedroom + armchair bed (3 places), sat TV, AC, washing machine; ideal for a couple with a child.
- **MorfeoCentro42** (vicolo Morfeo 42, Marina Corta, centro storico): historic building next to the Marina Corta piazza, near the Castello, Cattedrale, Museo Archeologico.
  - **Morfeo 1**: ~85 m², 2nd floor, kitchen with sofa bed, 4-bed room, second room with French bed; 6–8 guests.
  - **Morfeo 2**: ~85 m², 2nd floor, kitchen, 3/4-bed room with balcony, 5-bed room with wooden mezzanine; up to 9 guests.
  - Morfeo 1 + 2 share a ~150 m² terrace over the sea, Marina Corta and the Castello (washing machine and drying space on the terrace); can be rented together for 14–16 people.
  - **Morfeo 3** (built 2024): ground floor open space on the vicolo, Scavolini kitchen, double sofa bed, mezzanine with two singles, AC, dishwasher, washing machine, Wi‑Fi, outdoor table; up to 4 guests; pastry workshop ~10 m away.
  - Beach ~600 m on foot; bus stop 20 m; boats for the other six islands leave from Marina Corta.
Success = a visitor sends an availability request with dates, number of adults/children and preferred flat.

## Positioning
Owners who are Aeolian ("eoliani doc") and answer personally: they suggest which flat fits, how to get there, where to eat, which excursions. Two very different locations: one literally on the water at Marina Lunga, one in the historic centre with a huge terrace over Marina Corta.
